# LUNEVRA Technologies — Founder's Handbook

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Foundational internal document  
**Tagline:** Independent by design. Built to create.

## 1. Company overview

LUNEVRA Technologies is an independent, founder-led technology studio focused on developing software products, digital applications and interactive entertainment.

The company brings together engineering and creativity to produce distinctive digital experiences. It begins as a small independent operation, with the ambition to grow sustainably through original products, technical innovation and consistent quality.

LUNEVRA is currently operated by one founder. Company materials must not imply that it has employees, offices, investors, clients or departments that do not exist.

## 2. Founding vision

Build an independent technology company that creates meaningful digital products and memorable interactive experiences.

Good technology should not merely function. It should be thoughtfully designed, carefully engineered and enjoyable to experience.

## 3. Core principles

- **Quality over quantity:** Every release should meet a meaningful standard.
- **Creative independence:** Develop original ideas without unnecessary limitations.
- **Engineering excellence:** Prioritise maintainability, performance and reliability.
- **User experience:** Build products around the people who use them.
- **Sustainable growth:** Expand only when capabilities and needs justify it.
- **Long-term thinking:** Create products and intellectual property that can evolve.

## 4. Organisational structure

LUNEVRA initially operates as a single-founder studio. The founder oversees product development, engineering, creative direction, business strategy and company operations.

The company uses three internal divisions:

### LUNEVRA Digital

Consumer applications, software products and digital utilities.

Initial product:
- **LUNA** — an adaptive music player whose visual personality responds to the music being played.

### LUNEVRA Interactive

Video games, interactive storytelling and original entertainment IP.

Initial projects:
- **Twice-Told Tale** — released visual novel.
- **What the Water Keeps** — atmospheric action-adventure game in development.

### LUNEVRA Labs

Experimental technologies, research, prototypes and internal innovation. Initially an informal research function rather than a separate team or legal entity.

## 5. Product philosophy

Every LUNEVRA product should demonstrate:

1. A clear purpose and identity.
2. Thoughtful user-interface and interaction design.
3. Reliable technical foundations.
4. Consistent visual and functional quality.
5. Proper documentation and maintenance.
6. An honest and distinctive value proposition.

## 6. Brand identity

**Primary identity:** LUNEVRA Technologies  
**Positioning:** Independent Software & Interactive Entertainment Studio.

Visual direction:
- Near-black and graphite foundations.
- Restrained silver and cyan accents.
- Minimalist, contemporary typography.
- Architectural and futuristic visual language.
- Understated, sophisticated presentation.

Brand personality: mysterious, professional, inventive and confident.

Avoid generic purple gradients, excessive neon and generic startup aesthetics.

## 7. Founder responsibilities

The founder is responsible for:
- Establishing company direction.
- Managing the product portfolio.
- Overseeing engineering and creative development.
- Maintaining documentation and repositories.
- Managing releases and quality assurance.
- Establishing partnerships and business relationships.
- Maintaining financial and operational discipline.

## 8. Growth strategy

### Phase I — Foundation
- Establish corporate identity.
- Organise existing projects.
- Establish official digital presence.
- Publish a professional product portfolio.

### Phase II — Product development
- Improve and maintain existing products.
- Release upcoming projects.
- Develop new original software and entertainment IP.

### Phase III — Sustainable expansion
- Establish revenue streams.
- Build an audience and customer base.
- Explore collaborations.
- Introduce additional contributors when justified.

### Phase IV — Long-term development
- Expand the product ecosystem.
- Establish specialised teams where necessary.
- Explore new technology markets.
- Develop LUNEVRA into a recognised independent technology company.

## 9. Operating philosophy

LUNEVRA will remain lean, independent and quality-driven during its early stages.

No division, subsidiary or corporate position should exist purely for appearance. The company's structure should reflect its actual capabilities and operational requirements.

## 10. Closing statement

Create things worth experiencing. Build things worth maintaining. Never stop exploring what is possible.

*Independent by design. Built to create.*
